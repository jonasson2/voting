Denmark Folketing election, 24 March 2026
Generator: data/denmark/get-votes.py
Run: uv run --locked python data/denmark/get-votes.py 2026
Vote file: data/denmark_2026.csv
Official votes and seats: data/denmark/official-results_2026.json
Format and checks: data/denmark/README.md
All 12 parties and 6 independent candidates are retained, with original names
and unmodified votes. The 10 constituencies sum to 3,567,625 valid votes,
135 fixed seats and 40 adjustment seats. Regional adjustment totals are
12 (Hovedstaden), 14 (Sjaelland-Syddanmark), 14 (Midtjylland-Nordjylland).
The file is registered as a simulator preset, excluding Faroe Islands and Greenland.
