https://resultat.val.se/data/resultat/val2026/RD_S.json
https://www.val.se/valresultat-och-statistik/riksdags--region--och-kommunval/valresultat-2026
The official final-results feed reports the eight separately listed parties in
each constituency and a combined total for other registered parties. The
generated file places that combined total in Pruned, retaining it for threshold
denominators without representing it as a fictional party.

Denmark Folketing elections, 5 June 2019, 1 November 2022, and 24 March 2026
Generator: data/denmark/get-votes.py
Run: uv run --locked python data/denmark/get-votes.py YEAR
Vote files: data/denmark_2019.csv, data/denmark_2022.csv, data/denmark_2026.csv
Official votes and seats: data/denmark/official-results_YEAR.json
Format and checks: data/denmark/README.md
Each file retains original party and independent-candidate names and
unmodified votes. They have 10 constituencies, 135 fixed seats and 40
adjustment seats, and exclude Faroe Islands and Greenland.
