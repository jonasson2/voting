Danish allocation discussion
============================

This note preserves the agreed Danish-law procedure and implementation plan.
The implementation now includes regions, independent candidates, the Denmark
preset, both allocation tables, and the 2026 mainland data. Tests cover the
official 2026 allocations and the 2022 recalculation of national party totals.
The statutory advance-allocation procedure remains deferred as specified below.

Basic structure
---------------

The Danish part of the Folketing has 175 seats: 135 fixed seats and
40 compensatory seats. The four seats elected in the Faroe Islands and
Greenland are outside this allocation.

Denmark has three regions (landsdele) containing ten constituencies
(storkredse). A lower geographical level is the nomination district
(opstillingskreds). The simulator should use the English labels "region",
"constituency", and "nomination district"; "multi-member" is unnecessary in
the constituency label.

The three regions are:

* Hovedstaden
* Sjælland-Syddanmark
* Midtjylland-Nordjylland

Verified allocation procedure
-----------------------------

### 1. Allocation of fixed seats

Allocate the fixed seats separately in each constituency using
d'Hondt divisors 1, 2, 3, and so on. Parties and independent candidates both
participate. There is no electoral threshold at this stage. Exact quotient
ties are decided by lot under Danish law.

### 2. Eligibility for compensatory seats

An independent candidate is not eligible for compensatory seats. A party is
eligible if it satisfies at least one of these conditions:

1. It obtained at least one fixed seat.
2. In at least two of the three regions, its regional vote is at least the
   total number of valid votes in that region divided by the number of fixed
   seats in that region.
3. It obtained at least 2 percent of all valid votes nationally.

For conditions 2 and 3, "all valid votes" includes votes for every party and
independent candidate, whether or not they qualify for compensatory seats. In
the simulator it also includes the retained totals for pruned parties. Pruned
parties and independent candidates contribute to these threshold bases but do
not thereby participate in the later allocation of compensatory seats.

### 3. Initial calculation of national party totals

Allocate the available seats among the eligible parties using the Hare quota
and largest remainders. The available number is the total number of seats
(normally 175) minus fixed seats won by independent candidates. Only
eligible-party votes enter the Hare quota, and the fixed seats already
won by parties are not minimum allocations. Exact ties are decided by lot.

### 4. Recalculation of party totals

Section 77(4)-(5) describes a new calculation and, if needed, a new
distribution. Fixed seats are not withdrawn.

Let f[p] be party p's fixed seats and t[p] its national total from
step 3. Keep f[p] fixed and recalculate t[p] as follows.

a) If no party has f[p] > t[p], accept the step-3 totals and finish.
b) Otherwise, set the final total of every party with f[p] >= t[p] to f[p]
   and remove those parties from further calculations.
c) Subtract all removed parties' final totals from the party-seat pool in
   step 3. Allocate the remaining seats among the remaining parties using
   their votes and Hare/largest remainders, updating t[p].
d) If no remaining party's t[p] exceeds its original step-3 total, accept
   these totals and finish.
e) Otherwise, fix each such party at its original step-3 total, remove it,
   and repeat c)-e) until d) ends the procedure.

Each party's compensatory seats equal its final total minus f[p].

### 5. Allocation of adjustment seats to regions

a) Sum each party's votes and fixed seats within each region.
b) Allocate adjustment seats using Maximum constituency votes with
   Sainte-Lague, treating regions as constituencies and starting from the
   fixed seats already allocated.
c) Enforce both each party's adjustment-seat entitlement from stage 4 and
   each region's specified adjustment-seat total.

If allocation cannot be completed because a party has no votes in some
regions, report a clear error. The statutory advance-allocation procedure
is deferred.

### 6. Allocation of adjustment seats to lists

a) Within each region, use Maximum constituency votes with the Danish
   divisor rule (1, 4, 7, ...), starting from the fixed seats already allocated.
b) For each seat, select the party-constituency pair with the highest
   quotient, considering only parties that still lack adjustment seats
   assigned to them in that region by stage 5. Give that party one seat in
   the selected constituency and update its quotient. Resolve exact ties
   by lot.
c) Repeat until every party has received its assigned adjustment seats in
   the region.

Step-by-step tables
-------------------

Use the two existing step-by-step tables, with one row per adjustment seat
in each table. Record the actual allocation decisions as they happen.

First table: Allocation of adjustment seats to regions (stage 5)

Columns: Adjustment seat # | Region | Party | Votes | Divisor | Vote score

Each row identifies the selected party and region. Votes are the party's
regional votes; Divisor is the Sainte-Lague divisor used for that seat;
Vote score is Votes / Divisor, calculated before awarding the seat. Rows
follow the allocation order across all regions.

Second table: Allocation of adjustment seats to constituencies (stage 6)

Columns: Adjustment seat # | Region | Constituency | Party | Votes |
         Divisor | Vote score

Each row identifies the selected party and constituency, together with its
region. Votes are the party's constituency votes; Divisor is the Danish
divisor used for that seat; Vote score is Votes / Divisor, calculated before
awarding the seat. Process regions in vote-table order and retain the seat
allocation order within each region.

Number the seats consecutively from 1 in each table. Both tables describe
the same adjustment-seat total at different geographical levels; their seat
numbers are independent and do not identify the same individual seat.
Mark any allocation decided by lot in the relevant row.

Input-table direction
---------------------

The vote table remains two-dimensional: one row per constituency and one
column per party. Add a region identifier to each constituency rather than
introducing separate vote-table panels. A small region table below the main
table can map identifiers to names and hold the number of compensatory seats
assigned to each region.

Use the flexible adjustment-seat representation already introduced for
Sweden:

* minimum adjustment seats in every constituency: 0
* maximum adjustment seats in every constituency: blank/infinity
* exact national adjustment-seat total: 40

Region-level totals add an intermediate constraint: the 40 compensatory
seats are first divided among the three regions and subsequently among the
constituencies in each region.

National party votes should be disallowed when region data are present. The
regional and national calculations should use sums of constituency votes.

Independent candidates need an explicit representation in real-election
input because they do not participate like nationwide parties. They should
be excluded from generated simulations. The file marker is an optional
Independent candidates row of 0/1 values. In a Danish single election each
independent can win at most one fixed seat in its constituency and cannot
receive adjustment seats.

Electoral-system pipeline
-------------------------

The general pipeline has four stages:

1. Allocate fixed seats in constituencies.
2. Apportion total seats to parties, applying any selected national special
   rules. The Danish rules include the statutory recalculation; the Swedish
   rules include subsequent reallocation of excess fixed seats.
3. If regions are present, allocate each party's adjustment-seat entitlement
   among regions.
4. Allocate adjustment seats to constituency lists, observing any regional
   totals.

There must be only one adjustment-seat allocator. The former secondary or
"additional" allocator has been removed. The primary allocator must either
handle flexible constituency bounds itself or reject them before allocation.
This is the right foundation for the future Danish stage 5.

Implementation plan
-------------------

Implement in the following four increments, beginning with the input format
and tab-1 controls so the representation can be inspected before connecting
the calculations. The six-stage procedure above defines the Danish rules;
the headings below describe implementation work.

### 1. Vote data and tab 1

Add optional region identifiers to constituencies, a region table containing
abbreviation, name and adjustment-seat count, and an independent-candidate
flag for each party/candidate column.

Provide a Region column and a small editable region table below the votes.
Add an Independent checkbox to the party-name table.

Extend CSV/Excel import and vote-table downloads with the region column,
region table, and an optional Independent candidates metadata row. Preserve
all metadata in download-all. The prepared data/denmark_2026.csv demonstrates
the file layout, documented in data/denmark/README.md: region follows max_adj,
independent flags align with vote columns, and the region table follows a
blank row headed Regions,Name,adj.

Validate identifiers, assignments and seat totals in frontend and backend.
Regional adjustment-seat totals must sum to the vote-table adjustment-seat
total, and constituency bounds must permit each region's total. Disallow
National party votes when regions are present. Existing files without regions
continue to work; seat counts remain configurable.

### 2. Electoral-system settings

Add Denmark to the Election-law preset selector with these settings:

* Fixed-seat rule: D'Hondt; local threshold: 0%.
* Party-total rule: Hare quota; adjustment threshold: 2% OR 1 fixed seat.
* Preparation method: Danish allocation to regions; rule: Sainte-Lague.
* Adjustment allocation method: Maximum constituency votes; rule: Danish.
* Seat specification: Refer to vote table.

The Danish special-rules switch activates both the two-region qualification
and the statutory party-total recalculation. With it off, retain fixed seats
and apportion the remaining seats using the selected rule. Danish preparation
then distributes adjustment seats among regions. Only applying the election-law
preset changes the other settings.

### 3. Allocation and results

Keep Danish eligibility and party-total recalculation in a small dedicated
backend module called from the existing party-total calculation.

For regional preparation, aggregate votes and fixed seats by region and
reuse Maximum constituency votes with exact regional adjustment-seat totals.
Store party-by-region entitlements separately from constituency allocations.

For final allocation, run Maximum constituency votes separately within each
region using those entitlements and the selected divisor rule. Combine the
results into the existing constituency-by-party result matrix.

Handle independent candidates in fixed-seat allocation, threshold bases, and
the national party-seat pool. Exclude zero-vote pairs and report a clear error
when the deferred advance-allocation procedure is needed. Support drawing
lots for ties with reproducible randomness for tests. Reject unsupported
combinations of regional preparation and final allocation methods.

Produce the two step-by-step tables specified above directly from allocation
records, including Excel output.

### 4. Simulation, presets and verification

Prepare a simulation copy of the vote table with independent candidates
removed and their votes retained in Pruned. Use that same copy for simulation
baseline calculations and generated elections.

Apply the agreed minimum-one-vote convention to participating lists in Danish
simulations, including the generator's starting data. Recompute eligibility,
national totals and regional allocations for every election.

Add a Danish data preparation script under data/denmark/, record sources in
data/sources.txt, and add official election presets and expected results.
Include the national 2022 totals to exercise this recalculation, alongside the
complete newest-election data.

Tests must cover:

* File round trips, malformed metadata, pruning, and party/constituency edits.
* All eligibility routes, independent votes, and party-total recalculations.
* Regional and national seat totals, constituency bounds, zero votes and ties.
* Both step-by-step tables and their Excel exports.
* Official Danish results, simulations and existing Nordic regressions.

Run the backend suite and frontend checks, and inspect the changed tabs in
the browser, including narrow-screen layouts.
