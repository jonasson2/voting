Danish allocation discussion
============================

This note preserves the design discussion for a future Danish-law
implementation. No Danish implementation has been started yet.

Basic structure
---------------

The Danish part of the Folketing has 175 seats: 135 constituency seats and
40 compensatory seats. The four seats elected in the Faroe Islands and
Greenland are outside this allocation.

Denmark has three regions (landsdele) containing ten constituencies
(storkredse). A lower geographical level is the nomination district
(opstillingskreds). The simulator should use the English labels "region",
"constituency", and "nomination district"; "multi-member" is unnecessary in
the constituency label.

The three regions are:

* Hovedstaden
* Sjælland-Syddanmark
* Midtjylland-Nordjylland

Input-table direction
---------------------

The vote table remains two-dimensional: one row per constituency and one
column per party. Add a region identifier to each constituency rather than
introducing separate vote-table panels. A small region table below the main
table can map identifiers to names and hold the number of compensatory seats
assigned to each region.

Use the flexible adjustment-seat representation already introduced for
Sweden:

* minimum adjustment seats in every constituency: 0
* maximum adjustment seats in every constituency: blank/infinity
* exact national adjustment-seat total: 40

Region-level totals add an intermediate constraint: the 40 compensatory
seats are first divided among the three regions and subsequently among the
constituencies in each region.

National party votes should be disallowed when region data are present. The
regional and national calculations should use sums of constituency votes.

Independent candidates need an explicit representation in real-election
input because they do not participate like nationwide parties. They should
be excluded from generated simulations. The exact file marker and
single-election handling remain to be designed.

Electoral-system pipeline
-------------------------

The agreed general pipeline has five stages:

1. Allocate fixed seats in constituencies.
2. Apportion total seats to parties.
3. Apply any required overhang rule while determining feasible party totals.
4. Prepare for adjustment-seat allocation.
5. Allocate adjustment seats to constituency lists.

The fourth tab-2 heading is "Prepare for adjustment-seat allocation" and the
fifth is "Allocation of adjustment seats to lists".

For ordinary existing systems, stage 4 is "Not applicable". For Sweden,
stage 4 performs Swedish switching. For Denmark, stage 4 will allocate each
party's compensatory-seat entitlement among the three regions. Stage 5 will
then place those seats in the constituencies while observing the region
totals.

There must be only one adjustment-seat allocator. The former secondary or
"additional" allocator is being removed. The primary allocator must either
handle flexible constituency bounds itself or reject them before allocation.
This is the right foundation for the future Danish stage 5.

National party totals and overhang
----------------------------------

After constituency seats and compensation eligibility have been determined,
the eligible parties' national seat totals are calculated by the Hare quota,
equivalently the largest-remainder method: allocate the integer parts of the
quotas and allocate the remaining seats in descending order of fractional
remainders.

At that point f[p] is party p's constituency-seat count and t[p] is its
calculated national total. If f[p] > t[p], the party keeps its constituency
seats, is fixed at f[p], and is removed from the next calculation. Recompute
the national totals for the remaining parties and remaining seats. Repeat
until every remaining party satisfies f[p] <= t[p]. An iteration can expose a
new overhang, so this is genuinely iterative.

The simulator normally describes party-total allocation as starting from
zero. That wording need not be added to the UI for Denmark; it is already the
normal interpretation of this stage.

The legal allocation after national party totals
-------------------------------------------------

The remaining statutory formulas still need to be transcribed and tested
carefully before implementation:

* allocation of each party's compensatory seats among the three regions;
* allocation from each region to its constituencies;
* the precise eligibility tests for compensatory seats;
* treatment of independent candidates and votes not participating in the
  compensatory allocation;
* statutory tie handling;
* the special advance-allocation provision in section 78 when a party's
  absence from a region would otherwise prevent filling its entitlement.

The section 78 rule resembles the simulator's historical forced-allocation
idea but has not been established as equivalent. The initial implementation
should report a clear single-election error if this exceptional provision is
needed. Generated simulations may assume that every simulated party has
positive votes in every constituency.

Implementation sequence
-----------------------

1. Finalize the region metadata and independent-candidate file format.
2. Add parsing, validation, editing, download, and round-trip tests for region
   data without changing election calculations.
3. Implement Danish fixed-seat allocation and compensation eligibility with
   small legal examples.
4. Implement iterative national Hare/largest-remainder totals and overhang
   handling as a separately tested operation.
5. Implement region allocation as a preparation method.
6. Extend the sole adjustment-seat allocator to enforce region totals and
   flexible constituency bounds.
7. Add official-election regression fixtures and simulation tests.

Before coding stages 3-6, verify every formula and the exceptional cases
against the current Danish election law or an authoritative official manual.
